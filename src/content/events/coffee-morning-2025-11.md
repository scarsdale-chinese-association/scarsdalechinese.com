In this season of gratitude, SCA warmly invited members and friends to our "Gratitude & Welcome: Connecting Through Volunteer Stories" Coffee Morning.

As the first Coffee Morning of the 2025–2026 SCA year, this gathering was dedicated to welcoming new neighbors and celebrating the many Chinese volunteers who make Scarsdale a caring and vibrant community.

Community volunteers from organizations such as the School Board, PTAs, CNC, SBNC, SFCS, Scarsdale Foundation, and Project Ready shared their stories and experiences, helping newcomers learn more about Scarsdale's volunteer organizations and how they work together to serve the community.
