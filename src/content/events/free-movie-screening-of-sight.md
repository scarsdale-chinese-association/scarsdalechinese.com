A special free screening of *Sight*, starring Terry Chen and Greg Kinnear. This compelling film tells the story of Dr. Ming Wang, whose pioneering work in vision restoration is made possible by the lessons of the past.

Following the screening there was an exclusive Q&A session with Dr. Wang, offering insights into the incredible real-life story behind the film and the importance of perseverance, innovation, and the human spirit.

The event was free and open to the public, sponsored by the Scarsdale Chinese Association and OCA-WHV.
