The Scarsdale Chinese Association invited the community to ring in the Year of the Horse at Reid Castle, Manhattanville University.

The festivities began at 4:00 PM and included live cultural performances, a festive Lunar New Year dinner, family-friendly activities and a kids' area, an AR Year of the Horse photo experience, New Year gift bags for every adult ticket, and a raffle with exciting prizes.

**Admission:** Adult $68 · Senior $58 · Ages 4–10 $55 · Under 3 free. RSVP was by February 20, 2026, with payment via Zelle to scarsdalechinese@yahoo.com (household name and ticket count by age group in the memo).
