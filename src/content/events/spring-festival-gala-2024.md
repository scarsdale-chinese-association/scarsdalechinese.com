A Celebration of Culture and Community. The community gathered on February 11, 2024 to usher in the Year of the Dragon with an unforgettable evening of celebration at the Scarsdale Chinese Spring Festival Gala 2024: a festive evening filled with performances, games, and delicious authentic Chinese food.

Whether you came for traditional music and dance performances, family-friendly games, or simply to enjoy authentic Chinese cuisine, there was something for everyone.

**Ticket prices:** Adults $55 · Seniors $45 · Children (5–12) $35 · Children 4 and under free.
