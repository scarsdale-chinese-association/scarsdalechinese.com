The Scarsdale Chinese Association and ALT Alliance presented *2024 New Year Melody*, a captivating concert showcasing the talents of young Chinese musicians, in the Scott Room at the Scarsdale Library.

The concert featured a delightful range of performances from up-and-coming musicians, celebrating the start of the new year through music. All proceeds were used to purchase Chinese books for donation to the Scarsdale Library, enriching the community's cultural and educational resources.
