About the Senior Club

The Senior Club, managed by the Village of Scarsdale, offers a welcoming space for senior citizens in our community. To join, register for an account with the Village of Scarsdale and pay the annual membership fee of $15.

Recognizing the unique needs of our Chinese-speaking senior residents, the Village of Scarsdale has partnered with the Scarsdale Chinese Association to provide tailored support and culturally enriching activities. Together, we organize events and programs designed specifically for Chinese senior citizens living in Scarsdale.

Our collaboration ensures that all senior residents, regardless of language, can engage, connect, and enjoy their golden years in a vibrant and supportive community.

Club life

Dance troupe, choir, qipao shows, potlucks and holiday parties. Here is a glimpse of what our members get up to.

FAQ

What is the SCA Senior Club?

The SCA Senior Club is a community group for Chinese-speaking senior citizens in Scarsdale. The official Senior Club is run by the Village of Scarsdale; SCA members organize their own culturally tailored activities within it.

How do I join?

Create an account on the Village of Scarsdale website (scarsdale.com), choose Seniors → Senior Club Membership, and pay the $15 annual fee. Then join us on Tuesday mornings at the Girl Scout House.

What kinds of activities are there?

Weekly social gatherings, singing and dancing, cultural celebrations, educational talks, and seasonal outings tailored to the interests of our Chinese senior members.

Can I also attend the Village's official Senior Club events?

Yes. Village Senior Club membership gives you access to all official Senior Club programs run by the Village of Scarsdale, in addition to the SCA activities.
