FAQ

What is the Scarsdale Chinese Association (SCA)?

SCA is a 501(c)(3) community organization dedicated to promoting Chinese culture, supporting local residents, and fostering community engagement in Scarsdale and the surrounding areas through cultural, educational, and social events.

Why should my business become a sponsor?

Sponsoring SCA connects your business with an engaged local community, raises your brand's visibility, and shows your commitment to cultural diversity. It also offers networking opportunities and access to a diverse audience of several hundred member families.

What types of sponsorship are available?

We offer event sponsorship, program sponsorship, and annual sponsorship packages. Each level comes with different benefits and recognition opportunities, and we are happy to tailor a package to your goals.

What are the benefits of sponsoring SCA?

Depending on level, benefits include brand visibility on event materials, this website and social media; recognition in our newsletter and at events; complimentary event tickets; opportunities to speak or present at events; and logo placement on promotional materials.

Can I sponsor a specific event or program?

Yes. You can sponsor specific events or programs that align with your business interests, including the Spring Festival Gala, concerts, educational workshops, and community service activities.

Are donations tax deductible?

Yes. Scarsdale Chinese Association, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, so donations are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law. We will send a receipt for your records.

Are in-kind sponsorships possible?

Yes. We welcome in-kind contributions such as venue space, food and beverages, printing, or professional services, and recognize them in the same way as cash sponsorships.
