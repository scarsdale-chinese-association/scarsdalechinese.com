Hello Fall Festival

Date
Sunday, October 13, 2024
Time
2:30 – 5:30 PM
Location
Girl Scout House37 Wayside Lane, Scarsdale, NY 10583

Hosted by the Scarsdale Chinese Association at the Girl Scout House, the Hello Fall Festival welcomed new families and reunited old neighbors with bouncing houses, face painting, games, prizes, food, snacks and beverages.
