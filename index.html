About SCA

Scarsdale Chinese Association (SCA) is a non-profit, non-political organization committed to serving the community of Scarsdale, NY, by promoting the appreciation of Chinese culture and heritage, and by enhancing cross-cultural exchange and understanding.

Through educational seminars, cultural workshops, social gatherings and community activities, SCA provides opportunities for learning, networking and collaboration among its members and the wider Scarsdale community. Each year we hold lectures and seminars, a large Spring Festival Gala, youth concerts, festivals and coffee mornings.
